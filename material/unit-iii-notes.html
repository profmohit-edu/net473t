Classroom Caselet

Assume BVCOE is moving a student academic portal to cloud. Identify the asset, risk, responsible party, preventive control, detective control and audit evidence. This one case can be reused across all four units by changing the security layer being studied.

Must-Prepare Questions

  • Differentiate data at rest, in transit and in use.

  • Explain the data information lifecycle in cloud.

  • Compare privacy transformation techniques.

  • Explain cloud key management audit and best practices.

  • Map controls to data at rest, in transit and in use.

  • Compare masking, obfuscation, anonymization and tokenization.

  • Design a key-management lifecycle.

  • Prepare audit checks for data and KMS controls.

High-Scoring Answer Formula

  • Start with a precise definition.

  • Draw or describe architecture/lifecycle/pipeline where relevant.

  • Mention risk, control and audit evidence.

  • Use syllabus words directly.

  • Close with limitation or best-practice statement.
