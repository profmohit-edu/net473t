NET-473T_15_Mark_Answer_Bank

This document gives answer frameworks for expected long-answer areas in Cloud Computing and Security. Students should convert these frameworks into their own words and include diagrams/tables wherever suitable.

Standard 15-Mark Structure

  • Definition and context: 2 marks

  • Architecture/lifecycle/process explanation: 3 marks

  • Risks or challenges: 3 marks

  • Controls and best practices: 4 marks

  • Audit evidence or verification: 2 marks

  • Conclusion: 1 mark

Unit I: Cloud Concepts, Architecture and IAM

I.1 Cloud computing concepts and essential characteristics

  • Start by defining cloud computing concepts and essential characteristics in cloud-security context.

  • Explain the main components, stages, actors or mechanisms.

  • Add a simple diagram/table: architecture, lifecycle, comparison matrix, checklist or pipeline.

  • Discuss at least three risks or mistakes students/organizations make.

  • Recommend at least four controls or best practices.

  • Mention audit evidence: logs, access reviews, scan reports, approval trail, configuration baseline, key-use log, test report or monitoring alert.

  • End with one limitation or best-practice conclusion.

Model conclusion: In cloud computing, this topic must be handled as a shared-responsibility issue where technical control, process control and audit evidence together establish security assurance.
