Official Assessment Scheme

How the Theory Paper is Assessed

Teachers Continuous Evaluation carries 25 marks. The term-end theory examination carries 75 marks. The term-end paper has nine questions. Question 1 is compulsory for 15 marks and covers the complete syllabus through short/objective/single-line questions. Each unit has two alternative questions; students attempt one question from each unit, carrying 15 marks per unit.
